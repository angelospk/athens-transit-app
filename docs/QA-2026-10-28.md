### R1 · Delay alerts

- Alert sheet and rule editor: switch «Πες μου και αν αργεί». A bus of that line and direction, before your
  stop, with a delay of 5′ or more, gives one notification: «040 αργεί 7′ · Για τη στάση Χ: φτάνει σε ~10′».
- Once per bus run; it does not replace the arrival notification (separate notification id).
- Works for one-off and weekly rules.
- The delay is the server's (`delay_s`, from the GTFS trip match). With the expired GTFS the delay is
  only as good as the weekday match; R2 does not change this (the matcher still uses the GTFS).
- Found while doing it: the rule editor saved a «πότε να φύγω» weekly rule as a plain arrival rule
  (no `kind`), so the notification said «040 → στάση», not «Φύγε τώρα». Fixed: the editor keeps the kind
  and opens a leave rule on «πότε να φύγω».

### R2 · Daily OASA timetable

- Backend branch `daily-schedule` (merged into `mcp`): `/v1/lines/{id}/schedule` for today reads OASA
  `getDailySchedule` for every line code of the line (in parallel, 8 s in total), merged and sorted;
  `source: "oasa-daily"`. Cached for the day; a partial answer (one code failed) is asked again after 10′.
  Fallback: the GTFS timetable (`source: "gtfs"`, with `expired`).
- In the app: «Δρομολόγια» of a line shows today's OASA times, note «Πρόγραμμα ημέρας ΟΑΣΑ». No
  struck-through («δεν φάνηκε») times then: the OASA runs are not matched to buses.
- Not changed: the trip matcher, `delay_s` and the stats still use the GTFS (expired 2026-07-08).
- Checked on a local server: 608 in 4.5 s during bootstrap, Β9 36/36 runs (two vehicles at one minute
  are now one departure, Q9).

### R3 · ETA from live traffic

- Backend branch `live-speeds` (on top of `daily-prefetch`): every move of every bus between two GPS
  fixes, of any line, is recorded per 100 m of road and heading (8 directions). A road piece has a
  live speed when 2 or more buses crossed it in the last 20′. The speeds ahead of a bus
  (`path_speeds`, 200 m steps) become 0.7 × live + 0.3 × the usual speed at this hour/day type
  (the 14-day `segspeed` table); live alone where there is no history; the usual where nobody
  passed. A bus waiting at its terminus does not count.
- The app already moves buses between fixes with these speeds (Motion), so it needs no change.
  Contract rev 7 (same field, same units); copy `docs/CONTRACT.md` to athens-transit-map on merge.
- Checked: unit + server tests (two buses at 15 m/s where the usual is 5 → 12 m/s ahead); local server
  10′ at 06:00: ~4000 road pieces, 40 of 180 buses got live speeds ahead (median 17 km/h); a full
  1500-bus round costs 16 ms.
- Already there before: speed per shape × 200 m × hour × day type (`segspeed`), smooth movement
  between fixes (Motion).

### R4 · Runs not started yet at a stop

- Stop sheet: «Δεν ξεκίνησαν ακόμη · από το πρόγραμμα»: today's departure from the start + the run to
  the stop at mean speed, within 60′, after the last live bus of that direction, at most 2 per direction.
- Only with today's timetable (R2): with the expired GTFS the section stays hidden.
- At most the first 12 lines of a stop load a timetable (Q11).

### R5 · Worst lines over a period

- Στατιστικά › Τάσεις › «Χειρότερες γραμμές», 7 / 14 / 28 days: most missed runs (share) and slowest
  (km/h); a tap opens the line on the map. The days are as many as the server has (now 19).
- Delay per trip is not recorded by the server; «missed» is counted against the expired GTFS, and the
  screen says so.

- `live-speeds` fast-forwarded into `main` of athens-transit-rt (4208772); ci, image, gtfs green;
  `docker compose pull && up -d` in `~/services/atrt`. Contract rev 7 copied to athens-transit-map (38fd18c).
- Checked on production: `POST /mcp` initialize answers; the app (emulator, production API) shows
  «Πρόγραμμα ημέρας ΟΑΣΑ» under a line's «Δρομολόγια»; the planner and the stats tap work.
- Seen after the deploy: the pacer stayed at its slowest (0.62 of 5 rps, ~100 buses tracked,
  `road_cells` 40–90). Cause: the daily prefetch takes 1 in 4 slots, and every getDailySchedule answer
  (3–7 s, > 5 s «slow») doubled the backoff while a healthy answer shrinks it only ×0.9: 2 × 0.9³ > 1.
- Fix (c56ee3e, deployed 11:33 Athens; Codex APPROVE, CodeRabbit 0): a background job reports only its
  errors to the pacer, not its latency. After it: 5 rps, 0 errors, ~375 buses, `road_cells` 5862 at
  13′ and rising.
- MCP checked as a client on production: `tools/list` (8 tools), `find_stops` «Σύνταγμα» → stop 10341,
  `stop_arrivals` → 040 in 14′.
