### R1 · Delay alerts

- Alert sheet and rule editor: switch «Πες μου και αν αργεί». A bus of that line and direction, before your
  stop, with a delay of 5′ or more, gives one notification: «040 αργεί 7′ · Για τη στάση Χ: φτάνει σε ~10′».
- Once per bus run; it does not replace the arrival notification (separate notification id).
- Works for one-off and weekly rules.
- The delay is the server's (`delay_s`, from the GTFS trip match). With the expired GTFS the delay is
  only as good as the weekday match; R2 does not change this (the matcher still uses the GTFS).
- Found while doing it: the rule editor saved a «πότε να φύγω» weekly rule as a plain arrival rule
  (no `kind`), so the notification said «040 → στάση», not «Φύγε τώρα». Fixed: the editor keeps the kind
  and opens a leave rule on «πότε να φύγω».
