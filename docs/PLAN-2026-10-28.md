## Answers to Harold's questions

- **Where arrival times come from.** OASA's telematics give only positions. The minutes are ours:
  position along the line's shape, then the typical speed per 200 m at that day and hour
  (`path_speeds`, from the server's history), else 4.2 m/s. `~` marks an estimate without fresh data.
- **How the app knows you are on a bus.** Today: you say so («Είμαι μέσα»). Automatic: compare the
  phone's GPS track with the buses near it for 1-2 minutes (same direction and speed, distance < 40 m);
  needs background location, so it is a next stage.
- **Metro and tram times** are the STASY timetable (GTFS from the Growthfund catalogue, it expires
  2026-10-31: renew it), not live. Suburban rail is live (railway.gov.gr).
- **Google Maps transit**: no public API for Athens transit routing without a paid Directions key;
  scraping it breaks its terms. Not done.
